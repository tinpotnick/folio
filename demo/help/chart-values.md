# Reading a chart's values

- **Point at a line, area or bar chart** and a dashed guide marks the
  nearest point, with each series' exact value beside it - money as
  money (1,389.58), percentages as percentages, dates as dates. On a
  phone or tablet, tap the chart.
- **With the keyboard**: Tab to a chart (or search "read a chart's
  values" with Ctrl+K), then the left and right arrows step through it,
  Page Up and Page Down move a year (12 points) at a time, Home and End
  jump to the ends, and Esc puts it away. Screen readers
  hear each value.
- On a scatter or pie chart, rest the pointer on a point or slice to
  see its value.
- It is only a reading aid: the chart is unchanged, a click still opens
  its settings bar, and printed or exported pages do not show it.
