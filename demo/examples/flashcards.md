# Spanish for travellers: a flashcard deck

This document carries its own **flashcard deck** - a `::: flashcards`
block, plain Markdown like everything else. Click the deck's label to
open its bar and press **Study deck** for the session-only study modes
(Flip, Match, Choice); your grades live in this browser's local
storage, never in the file. Edit the cards right in the deck, or add a
deck from the Insert menu / Ctrl+K "flashcard deck".
